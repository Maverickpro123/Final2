About CYPG

Centre for Youth in Policy and Governance

The Centre for Youth in Policy and Governance (CYPG) is a youth-led institution established to strengthen meaningful youth engagement in governance, public policy, and national development processes. Founded in recognition of the existing gap between young people and institutional decision-making systems, CYPG serves as a platform for empowering youth with the knowledge, capacity, and exposure required to actively participate in shaping policy and development agendas.

CYPG operates as a non-partisan and independent organization committed to promoting informed, constructive, and responsible youth participation in governance and public discourse. The organization focuses on building a generation of young leaders who are not only aware of governance systems but are also capable of contributing to policy formulation, institutional development, and economic transformation.

At the core of CYPG's work is the belief that young people are not merely beneficiaries of development, but critical stakeholders and drivers of national progress. The organization therefore seeks to bridge the gap between youth, academic institutions, policymakers, and the private sector by creating structured pathways for engagement, dialogue, and collaboration.

Founder's Statement

The idea behind CYPG was born from a simple observation: many young people desire to shape society, yet few are prepared to understand the systems that govern it.

I founded CYPG on the conviction that sustainable participation in governance requires preparation before influence. When young people grasp institutional frameworks, policy processes, and regulatory systems, they engage more responsibly — moving from reactive discourse toward informed contribution.

CYPG is not simply a youth initiative. It is an intellectual platform designed to cultivate policy literacy, institutional awareness, and ethical leadership. Governance ecosystems thrive when generational continuity is nurtured deliberately.

Through CYPG, I lead efforts to create structured platforms for youth engagement in governance and economic development by facilitating policy discussions, promoting leadership development, and building networks between young people, academic institutions, policymakers, and private sector stakeholders.

— Shedrack Elisa Zadock, Founder & CEO, CYPG

Youth Participation in Decentralisation Reforms

Despite constitutional provisions for youth inclusion in local governance, structural barriers continue to limit meaningful participation. This brief examines the 2025 Local Government Act amendments and their implications for youth engagement.

Key findings: Only 11% of ward councillors are under 35, against a 20% target. The brief recommends a Youth Governance Fund, mandatory training for young councillors, and strengthened youth advisory structures.
